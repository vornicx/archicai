# Archic Public Quality Standard 2026.2

This document is the client-facing quality contract represented on the Archic website. It complements the internal `ARCHIC_QUALITY_STANDARD_2026.md`; it does not replace the broader internal rubric.

A project presented as an Archic delivery should make the following expectations verifiable.

## 1. Purpose-built mobile

Mobile is reviewed as its own experience. Hierarchy, navigation, touch targets, interactions, content density and image framing must work deliberately on small screens rather than merely shrinking desktop.

## 2. Critical journeys

The primary business flow must be tested end to end. Depending on the product this can include contact, booking, search, enquiry, authentication, private access, catalogue exploration or an operational workflow.

## 3. Performance

Images, fonts, JavaScript, loading behaviour and production assets are reviewed before launch. Performance is treated as part of perceived quality, not as an optional technical cleanup.

## 4. Accessibility

Contrast, keyboard access, focus visibility, semantic structure and reduced-motion behaviour are part of the final QA pass.

## 5. Search & AI readiness

Where relevant, the delivery includes a technically sound base for discovery: metadata, canonical URLs, crawlability, sitemap, structured data, semantic content and public AI discovery files when they provide value. This standard does not promise rankings or guaranteed visibility in AI products.

## 6. Content integrity

Archic does not invent clients, testimonials, awards, business results or success metrics to make a project appear more established. Concept builds and fictional demo data must be labelled as such. Business outcomes become case-study claims only when their source and measurement can be explained.

## 7. Visual QA

Desktop and mobile are reviewed on the rendered product, not only in source code or static design files. Visual regressions, overlap, clipping, broken responsive states and inconsistent spacing are blocking issues when they affect a critical screen.

## 8. Ownership & portability

The code and project assets should not intentionally trap the client inside a closed builder or proprietary delivery mechanism. Handover and portability remain part of the product quality model.
