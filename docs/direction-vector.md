# Direction Vector — Archic Website

Date: 2026-08-28
Foundation: 1.0.0
Primary archetype: **Editorial Spread**
Secondary archetype: **Dossier**
Lead medium: **Type + Data**

| Axis | Score | Decision |
|---|---:|---|
| Density | 4/10 | Enough information to prove capability; generous grouping prevents a dashboard feel. |
| Volume | 2/10 | Flat paper and ink. Depth is reserved for product semantics, never decoration. |
| Geometry | 7/10 | Strong rules, measured edges, square controls and deliberate asymmetry. |
| Temperature | 4/10 | Mineral paper adds humanity; black/gold identity stays exact and controlled. |
| Era | 6/10 | Contemporary system UI with editorial discipline, not retro print imitation. |
| Kinetics | 3/10 | One system switcher, short state transitions and calm reveals. |
| Distance | 3/10 | Direct commercial language and product detail without becoming visually noisy. |

## Signature interaction

The hero system switcher connects **Presence → Control → Business** to a changing proof panel. A visitor sees the relationship between public experience, private operations and business logic before scrolling. On mobile, the three choices become an equal-width tab row with the proof directly beneath it.

## Composition rules

- Editorial Spread owns major statements and chapter changes.
- Dossier rows own comparable systems, method, pricing and QA.
- The product interface is evidence, not a decorative device.
- Space establishes hierarchy before borders; borders mark structure only.
- Typography remains grotesk + mono on the public studio site. Decorative serif is excluded from this direction.

## Anti-slop registry

Explicitly rejected:

- black + serif + gold as a generic luxury-agency costume;
- giant claim + generic subtitle + two floating CTAs;
- feature-card grids and bento layouts;
- gradients, glows, glass, floating shapes and ornamental 3D;
- rounded controls used without a product reason;
- stock imagery presented as client proof;
- metrics without an explicit demo or source label;
- motion that hides content or delays input.

## Branding resolution

The current identity is preserved exactly through the canonical mark and `#0A0A0B` / `#C9A56A`. Small text on light surfaces uses a darker functional gold derivative to meet contrast; the canonical gold remains in the mark, progress signal and filled brand controls. The site avoids the old luxury shorthand by changing composition, typography, hierarchy and evidence—not by inventing a new Archic colour system.
