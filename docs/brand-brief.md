# Brand brief — Archic

Date: 2026-08-28
Owner: Archic
Status: approved project input

## 1. Business truth

Archic designs the public layer a customer sees and builds the private systems a team uses: digital presence, operational control and custom software. The value is not a style of website. It is clearer customer journeys, less operational friction and portable code owned by the client.

## 2. Audience

- Spanish small and mid-sized businesses with real operational complexity.
- Founders and operators who need commercial clarity before a technical specification.
- Teams outgrowing disconnected forms, spreadsheets, generic builders or manual hand-offs.
- Buyers who value direct access to the people doing the work, verifiable scope and long-term ownership.

## 3. Desired action

The primary action is to describe what should change in the business. The visitor should not need to choose a package or diagnose the technical solution before contacting Archic.

## 4. Promise hierarchy

1. Archic connects what the customer sees with what the team needs.
2. Product evidence is visible before process claims.
3. The project advances through verifiable decisions and a blocking quality standard.
4. Code and assets remain deliverable and portable.

## 5. Voice

- Precise: concrete objects, flows and outcomes instead of “innovation” language.
- Direct: short claims followed by evidence.
- Human: no synthetic scale, invented authority or inflated studio language.
- Bilingual by authorship: Spanish and English are composed, not mechanically mirrored.

## 6. Identity invariants

- Canonical symbol: `/brand/archic-symbol-2026.svg`.
- Canonical lockups: `/brand/archic-lockup-dark.svg` and `/brand/archic-lockup-light.svg`.
- Canonical colours: Archic black `#0A0A0B` and Archic gold `#C9A56A`.
- Legacy marks, `_archic` wordmarks and unrelated identity strategies are not used to represent the current studio.
- The gold is a signal and identity detail, not a substitute for visual direction.

## 7. Integrity constraints

- Demos use fictional data and say so visibly.
- Concept builds never imply a completed client commission or measured result.
- No invented clients, testimonials, awards, team size, revenue or outcome metrics.
- Legal identity data remains incomplete until the business entity, tax ID and address are formally available; placeholders must never be guessed.
